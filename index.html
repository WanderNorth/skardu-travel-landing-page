A local travel service for Skardu and beyond

Ilyas Skardu Travels & Tours is a travel and tourism service based in Skardu, Gilgit-Baltistan. We help visitors explore Skardu and the destinations around it, from nearby lakes and valleys to longer routes across northern Pakistan.

Every trip is different, so we start with what you want to see and build from there. Whether you are travelling with family, friends or on your own, we can help with the planning so you can spend your time enjoying the mountains.

Popular destinations

Six places travellers ask about most. Tell us which ones you want and we will fit them into one plan.

Skardu

The gateway to Baltistan, set beside the Indus with mountain views in every direction. A good base for day trips.

See Skardu tours

Shangrila Resort

A well-known lakeside stop on the road to Skardu, popular for slow afternoons and photographs.

Ask about a visit

Upper Kachura Lake

A quiet alpine lake surrounded by hills, suited to a relaxed half-day or full-day outing.

Plan a lake trip

Deosai National Park

One of the highest plateaus in the world, with wide open plains and wildflowers in the warmer months.

Ask about Deosai

Shigar Valley

A green valley of orchards and old villages, known for its historic architecture and mountain scenery.

Explore Shigar

Hunza Valley

A famous valley of forts, terraced fields and big peaks. Many travellers pair it with Skardu on one route.

Plan a trip to Hunza
